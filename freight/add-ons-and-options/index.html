    Add-ons, Account Options & Account Status¶

    Your UTX Freight system is configured by UTX: which add-ons are on, which order types you use, how invoices are numbered, what some roles are allowed to do, and whether the account is active or suspended. This page lists every add-on and account option, what each one changes in the application, and what happens when the account is suspended.

    Overview¶

    • UTX turns these on. Add-ons and account options are part of your system's configuration. There is no screen in the application where you can switch them on or off yourself. To change one, contact UTX support.
    • Off means hidden. When an add-on is off, its menu items, buttons, columns and form fields are not shown at all. Opening one of its pages directly sends you back elsewhere in the application (for example to the Orders page, or to the Charge Types list with the message Fuel surcharge addon is not enabled.).
    • Shipment mode sets the shape of the whole application: intermodal (containers, terminals, steamship lines) or ground (trailers, pickup and delivery).
    • Order types decide which kinds of orders you can create (for example Container Import and Container Export).
    • Role permissions widen or narrow what the Manager, Dispatch, Accountant and Customer roles can do, on top of the normal role rules in User Roles.
    • Account suspension is set by UTX. While the account is suspended, nobody can create, duplicate or upload new orders, but existing orders stay visible.

    Quick Start¶

    1. To find out whether a feature is available to you, look for its menu item or button. If it is not there, the add-on is probably off (or your role does not have access).
    2. To turn an add-on or option on or off, contact UTX support. Say which one, using the names on this page.
    3. If you see the red System Notice: Account Suspended banner, contact the UTX accounting department using the details in the banner. Your existing orders remain available while you sort it out.

    Access & Permissions¶

    Who What they can do
    UTX support Turns add-ons and options on and off in your system configuration, and suspends or reactivates the account
    Admin, Manager and all other roles Use whatever the add-ons and options switch on. Cannot see or change the configuration. See the suspension banner and the blocked buttons when the account is suspended

    Your own company settings

    The options on this page are managed by UTX. Your own company settings are in Settings, in the user menu (click your name at the top of the screen). See Application Settings.

    Add-ons¶

    Each add-on is switched on or off separately by UTX.

    Add-on What appears when it is on More
    Driver Login Links A icon (tooltip Click to copy) next to the delivery driver once the order is ready for the driver, and next to the return (pickup) driver once the container is ready for return. Clicking it copies the driver's Login Link Driver Login Links
    Fuel Surcharge The Fuel Schedule menu item (for users who can manage charge types), a Fuel Surcharge section with the Exempt from Fuel Surcharge checkbox on the customer form, the Auto compute fuel surcharges for this order field on the order form, and automatic fuel charges on import, export and transfer orders that are not yet completed or invoiced and have auto compute on. Fuel surcharge charge types (the monthly rates) are hidden from the Charge Types list and managed on the Fuel Schedule page instead Fuel Surcharge
    CN Rail tracking CN Rail container tracking: a red icon (tooltip Check container status) on orders picked up at a CN terminal, and the CN Tracking button on the Dispatch page Container Tracking
    Port of Montreal (Termont) tracking Termont (Port of Montreal) container tracking: a blue icon on orders picked up at a Termont terminal, and the Port Tracking button on the Dispatch page Container Tracking
    MGT (Racine / Cast) tracking MGT (Racine / Cast) container tracking: an orange icon on orders picked up at a Racine or Cast terminal, and the Port Tracking button on the Dispatch page Container Tracking
    Auto Orders Auto Orders: an Upload button next to Create a new order on the Orders page, and an AUTO ORDERS filter box (Processing, Need Review, Completed) for reviewing orders read from uploaded documents. Shown to users allowed to use Auto Orders (Admin, Manager, Dispatch) Auto Orders
    Warehouse The Warehouse menu group (Orders, Product, Location, Charges, Unit of Measure, Building), the Warehouse user role, the Warehouse and Intermodal & Warehouse customer services, and Warehouse / Intermodal filter buttons on the customer list and the Charge Types list Warehouse Overview
    Garage The Garage menu item (repair shop work orders, invoices and payments), the Garage user role and the Garage customer service Garage Overview
    Multi-Office Several offices in one system. Customers, terminals, equipment and other lists are shared by all offices, while orders, invoices and checks stay with their office.     With any of the three tracking add-ons on, you also get the Alerts button on the Dispatch page, the Container Alerts page, the Alert Email Subscriptions box in Application Settings and an Alerts column in each customer's people list. If both CN Rail tracking and MGT (Racine / Cast) tracking could apply to an order, the CN icon is shown first.

    Tags¶

    Tags are not in the add-on list. They are turned on when UTX sets up a list of tag names for your account. With tags configured you get:

    • A Tag / Group field on the order form.
    • A Default tag field on the customer form.
    • Tag filter buttons (one per tag, plus No Tag) on the Dispatch page, and tag buttons on the Orders page for intermodal accounts.

    Users cannot add, rename or delete tags. See Order Tags.

    Account Options¶

    These options are also set by UTX. The Default column is the product default; your account may be configured differently.

    Shipment Mode¶

    Value What it changes
    Intermodal (default) Container-based screens. The Orders and Dispatch lists show ETA, Storage (STO) / Demurrage (DEM), Size / Steamship / Weight, Container# / Book#/Seal# / Last Free Day, Vessel / ERD / Cutoff Date, Terminal / Pickup From / Pickup #, Chassis #, C.Status and Return To / Return Date columns, plus the Empty at Yard and Full at Yard counters at the top of the Orders page. The driver columns are Delivery and Return. The Orders page has the ORDER TYPE and CONTAINER STATUS filter boxes, and the Dispatch page has container status and terminal filters. The user menu includes Manage Container Sizes, Manage Container Statuses, Manage Steamship Lines and Manage Terminals. The Dispatch date filter offers Delivery, ETA, LFD and CUT
    Ground Trailer-based screens. The Orders and Dispatch lists show Pickup From and Pickup Date columns, Trailer# instead of Container#, Equipment # instead of Chassis #, and Delivery Date instead of Delivery/Pickup. The driver columns are Pickup and Delivery. The container columns, container counters and filters, and container menu items above are hidden. The Dispatch date filter offers Pickup and Delivery

    Order Types¶

    UTX sets the order types you can choose in the Job type list on the New order form. It also sets the buttons in the ORDER TYPE filter box on the Orders page (intermodal accounts) and the order type filters on the Dispatch page. The Dispatch page has filters for Import, Export, Transfer and Dry Van. Default: Container Import and Container Export.

    Order type
    Container Import
    Container Export
    Container Transfer
    Dry Van Move
    International Export

    Order number format No prefix / numbers start at 781000 Order numbers can start with a prefix chosen for your account, followed by a number that counts up from a starting value

    Order history On Shows View History in the order row menu on the Orders and Dispatch pages (for users allowed to audit orders; dispatchers need Can manage rates?). When off, View History is hidden. See Order Status Workflow
    Automatic statements Off Adds the Automatic Statement schedule for this customer section to the customer form and the Delivery Day / Next Date column and the statement day counters to Customer Reports. See Automatic Statements
    Detailed statements Off Customer statements list the orders under each invoice (container number, reference number and order total), in the on-screen statement and in the export. See Customer Statements
    Invoice number letter and year On Invoice numbers start with the first letter of the customer's name and the two-digit invoice year, for example A26 - 00123. When off, the invoice number is just the five-digit number, for example 00123

    Option Default Role When on When off
    Accountants can manage orders On Accountant Can create, edit and manage orders in their office Can only view orders (and still complete and edit Dispatched orders)
    Accountants can manage checks On Accountant Can manage checks No access to checks
    Managers can manage checks On Manager Can manage checks No access to checks
    Managers can view reports On Manager Can open the reports (analytics) pages No access to the reports pages
    Dispatchers can manage customers Off Dispatch Can manage customers and their people Cannot manage customers
    Dispatchers can manage terminals Off Dispatch Can manage terminals Cannot manage terminals
    Customers can manage orders Off Customer Can create orders from the customer portal, and edit or delete their company's orders while they are still New Can only view their company's orders. See Customer Portal

    Account Suspension¶

    UTX can suspend your company's account, for example for late payment. Suspension affects the whole system, for every user.

    Suspension Levels¶

    Level Name Message shown to users
    0 Active No banner (normal use)
    1 Level 1 - Late Payment Warning Your account has been temporarily suspended due to late payment.
    2 Level 2 - Account Suspended Your account is suspended due to payment issues.
    3 Level 3 - Service Restricted Your account services are restricted.
    4 Level 4 - Collections Your account is under collections review.
    5 Level 5 - Account Terminated Your account has been terminated.

    Every message is followed by the contact details of the UTX accounting department (email and phone).

    All levels block the same actions

    The level changes the name and message shown, not what is blocked. Levels 1 to 5 all block the same actions, listed below.

    What Users See¶

    • A red, dismissible banner titled System Notice: Account Suspended at the top of the Orders page (and of the dashboard, for users who land there). It shows the level name with the level number (for example Level 2 - Account Suspended (Level 2)), the message, and a Reason: line if UTX entered a reason.
    • The Create a new order button turns red with a icon. Clicking it opens the Account Suspended - Action Blocked window (Order Creation Disabled), which repeats the level, message and reason and, under Why is this blocked?, explains: New order creation has been disabled due to the current account suspension. To restore access to order creation, please contact your system administrator to resolve the suspension. Click Close to dismiss it.
    • With Auto Orders, the Upload button also turns red and opens the same window.

    What Is Blocked¶

    Action While suspended
    Create a new order Blocked. The button opens the Account Suspended - Action Blocked window. Opening the new order page directly sends you back to the Orders list
    Duplicate an order Blocked. No copies are created and you are returned to the Orders list
    Upload (Auto Orders) Blocked. The button opens the Account Suspended - Action Blocked window
    Viewing, searching and opening existing orders Still allowed
    Editing, dispatching and completing existing orders, invoicing Not blocked by the suspension

    Lifting a Suspension¶

    Only UTX can suspend or reactivate the account. When the account is unsuspended, the level goes back to Active, the banner disappears and order creation, duplication and upload work again.

    FAQ / Troubleshooting¶

    A feature in these docs is missing from my menu¶

    Solution

    The feature is probably an add-on that is off for your account, or your role does not have access. Check the add-on table above, then ask your admin about your role. To turn an add-on on, contact UTX support.

    Can I switch an add-on or option myself?¶

    Solution

    No. Add-ons, shipment mode, order types, tags and role permissions are part of the system configuration that UTX manages. Contact UTX support with the name of the option you want changed.

    The Create a new order button is red¶

    Solution

    Your company's account is suspended. Read the banner at the top of the Orders page for the level and reason, then contact the UTX accounting department using the details shown. You can keep viewing existing orders in the meantime.

    Duplicate does nothing and I'm back on the Orders list¶

    Solution

    Duplicating is blocked while the account is suspended. No copies were created. Once the suspension is lifted, duplicate the order again.

    A dispatcher cannot edit customers or terminals¶

    Solution

    By default, dispatchers cannot manage customers or terminals. If your dispatchers need this, ask UTX support to turn on Dispatchers can manage customers and Dispatchers can manage terminals for your account.

    Best Practices¶

    Tips

    • When you ask UTX to change an option, name it exactly as on this page (for example Customers can manage orders) to avoid confusion.
    • Before turning on Customers can manage orders, decide who on your team will check the orders customers place from the portal.
    • Changing Invoice number letter and year changes how invoice numbers look. Agree on it before you start sending invoices, so numbers stay consistent for your customers.
    • Act on a Level 1 late payment warning quickly: order creation is already blocked at Level 1.
