    UTX Freight Documentation¶

    Welcome to the official documentation for UTX Freight, transport management software for freight companies. You use it to take orders, dispatch drivers, track containers, and invoice customers.

    About UTX Freight¶

    • Two shipment modes. Each UTX Freight account runs in either intermodal mode or ground mode. The mode is set for your account by UTX, not by users.
    • Optional modules. Garage (a repair shop module for work orders, parts and garage invoices) and Warehouse (a storage module for warehouse orders, products and locations) are optional modules. They only appear in the side menu when they are enabled for your account.
    • One app, several kinds of users. Office staff use the web app. Drivers, brokers and yard workers use the mobile screens. Customers and consignees can sign in to see their own orders.

    Add-ons that may not be on for your account

    Some features are add-ons that UTX turns on for your account. If one is not enabled, its menu items and buttons do not appear. Add-ons include:

    Fuel Surcharge, Container Tracking, Multi-office, Garage, Warehouse and Auto Orders (orders created from an uploaded document).

    Tagging (order tags) works the same way, but it is an account option rather than an add-on: it is on when UTX sets up a list of tag names for your account.

    Pages about these features say if enabled for your account. See Add-ons, Account Options & Account Status for the full list and what each one adds.

    Open these docs from inside UTX Freight

    The Online Documentation link in the top menu bar of the web app opens this site in a new tab.

    New Account? Start Here¶

    If you are an Admin or Manager, the side menu shows a setup checklist entry at the top:

    • Getting started on a new account (hover over it to see how many steps are left before your first invoice)
    • Setup check on an account that is already invoicing but still has items to set up (hover over it to see how many are left)
    • Setup complete with a green check once everything on the checklist is done

    Click it to see each remaining step and jump straight to the page where you complete it. See Setup Checklist and Getting Started.

    Not sure what a word or status means?

    The Glossary lists the terms, screen labels, abbreviations and status names used in UTX Freight (for example LFD, App. Taken or POD), with a short definition and a link to the page that explains each one. It also groups the alternate names some features go by.

    Container tracking is scheduled, not real-time

    When Container Tracking is enabled, UTX Freight checks the tracking sources your account is connected to on a schedule and updates container information when it runs. Updates are not instant. See Container Tracking.

    What's New¶

    Release 3.12 Invoice Filtering & Consignee Visibility

    The current production version is 3.12.19 (released 2026-09-17). The version you are running is shown as Release at the bottom of the side menu.

    • Invoice exports match your filters, with a choice of QuickBooks columns or AR aging detail layouts
    • Invoice date range and AR aging filters that combine with the other invoice filters, plus a Balance column and a days-past-due aging badge next to each due date
    • Consignee order visibility: a consignee linked to a customer account gets read-only visibility of orders delivered to them

    Need Help?¶

    Click Feedback (speech bubble icon) in the top menu bar of the web app to send a message to the UTX Freight development team. The We'd love your feedback window opens:

    1. Under What type of feedback?, choose General (the default), Bug Report or Suggestion.
    2. Under How important is this?, choose Low, Normal (the default) or Urgent.
    3. Write your message in Your Feedback (at least 10 characters, up to 2,000). Send Feedback stays greyed out until you reach 10 characters.
    4. Click Send Feedback, or Cancel to close the window without sending.

    The page you were on is sent along with your message for context.

    Where the Feedback button is

    Feedback (and the Online Documentation link) is only in the top menu bar of the main web app. The Garage pages and the mobile screens do not have it. To send feedback from there, open a page in the main web app first.

    See Signing In, Navigation & Your Profile for more about the top menu bar, the side menu and your profile.
